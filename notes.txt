Ideas for design and contents:

AllRecipe
  - A news-inspired recipe site that curates trending recipes from around the web.
  - Different sections, such as "Trending Now," "Editor's Picks," and "Seasonal Favorites."
  - User accounts for saving favorite recipes and creating shopping lists.
  - Integration with social media for sharing recipes.
  - Cluttered, magazine-style layout with lots of images and headlines.
  - Monetization through ads and sponsored content.
  - Search function without auto-suggestions - Not what we want. 


Bigoven
  - Quite outdated design.
  - Focuses on a large database of recipes with user reviews and ratings.
  - Categories such as "Low Carb", "Low Sodium", and "Diabetic Friendly."
  - Meal planner with a calendar view of planned meals with Breakfast, Lunch and Dinner.
  - Grocery list generator based on selected recipes.
  - "Ideas" section for recipe inspiration based on ingredients or meal types. Also guides on how to do more complex cooking techniques (e.g., making sourdough bread).
  - A really unique, "Use Up Leftovers" feature that suggests recipes based on 3 ingredients you have on hand.
